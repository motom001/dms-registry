Packages for the DMS

Ready-made Mappe types and views for your DMS – open source and approved by the DMS project. Install them in the DMS under Werkbank → Pakete (Workbench → Packages) or right from here.

Note: Package names and descriptions come from the packages themselves and are in German for now, as are the package pages (multilingual package content will follow). Where the German and English wording differ, the German one applies.
